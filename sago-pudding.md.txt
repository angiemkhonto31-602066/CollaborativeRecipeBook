Ingredients 

Sago 1 cup
Milk 4 cups
Butter 5 tablespoons 
Sugar 1/2 cup 
Eggs 4 separated
Vanilla essence 2 teaspoons
Salt 1/2 teaspoon

Instructions 

Prep: Preheat oven to 180°C (or 160°C fan-assisted) and grease a large ovenproof dish.
Soak Sago: Soak the sago in water for 15 minutes, or in the milk for at least 2 hours for a creamier texture.
Cook Sago: Bring the milk (and soaked sago) to a boil in a saucepan.
Reduce heat to low and simmer for 15–20 minutes, stirring constantly until the sago is transparent.
Add Flavorings: Remove from heat and stir in the butter, sugar, and vanilla.
Add Eggs: Allow to cool slightly, then stir in the egg yolks.
Bake: Pour into the dish. Optionally top with dollops of apricot jam. 
Bake for 30–45 minutes until set and golden brown on top.
Serve: Serve hot or warm, often with extra custard or cream
